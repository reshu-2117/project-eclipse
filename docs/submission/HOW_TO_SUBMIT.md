# How to submit Project Eclipse to NASA Space Apps 2026

Source: the official Project Submission Guide, https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/

**Deadline: Sunday 15 November 2026, 11:59 pm, local time of your Local Event.** Anything saved or submitted later is not judged.

The NASA site has no document upload. You paste text and links into the form on your team's **Project** tab. `Project_Eclipse_Components_of_Project_2026.docx` holds the text for every field.

## Before you open the form
1. **Demo link must open with no login.** Use the PDF in this repo: `https://github.com/reshu-2117/project-eclipse/blob/main/docs/submission/demo-deck/Project_Eclipse_Demo_Deck.pdf` (works once this work is merged to `main`). Test it in a private window.
2. The deck must be 7 slides or fewer (it is 7), in English, and show the team name, member names and the challenge (the cover does).
3. No person under 18 may appear (image, video, audio, likeness) in the demo or project. Ours has screenshots only.
4. Everyone on the team must be a confirmed participant at the **same Local Event**, and the team has at most six people.

## In the form (Profile icon, My Team, Project tab, blue Edit button)
Save often with "Save Progress". Turn the edit Lock on while you type.

| Form question | Paste from the report section |
|---|---|
| Name of your project | "Project Name": Project Eclipse: A Space Mission Design Game Where Every Choice Costs Something |
| High-level summary | High-Level Summary |
| Demo link (7-slide deck or 30 s video) | Demo Link (7-slide deck) |
| Link to your final project | Project Link: https://project-eclipse-new.vercel.app |
| Project details | Project Details (all five questions) |
| Super Intelligence (AI) used? | Use of AI. Not shown to judges, but be accurate |
| NASA data and resources (needed for a Global Award) | NASA Data |
| All data, resources and tools | Space Agency Partner Data and Other Resources |

Images can be pasted or uploaded (JPG, PNG, GIF, up to 10 MB). Videos and documents cannot, so link them.

## Submit
1. Tick "Terms and Conditions" and "Confirm All Work is Original".
2. Click **Submit for Judging**. A banner confirms it. You can edit and resubmit until the deadline; only the last submission is judged.
3. Download the participant certificate from Profile, Participant Information, 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge.

## Recommended
Ask NASA's FortiGuard filter to classify `project-eclipse-new.vercel.app` (Category: web-based application, Company: N/A) so judges' networks do not block it.
