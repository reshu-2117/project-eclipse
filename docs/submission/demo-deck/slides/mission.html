The mission

Lantern 17 is failing. You fly out to find out why.

On a planet with no sun, 24 orbital Lanterns light the cities. One is down to 61%, and a huge ice fragment is coming. You are one engineer among 300 crews.

The one rule

Every choice gains something and costs something, and the costs come from real physics: the rocket equation, orbital transfers, comms time, light pressure.

Build the ship at the Wick: hull, engine, power, shield, sensors, suits. Δv, mass, energy and budget change as you pick, and a readiness review flags what will hurt you later.

The player starts in the hangar. Every part has a real cost and a real benefit, and the numbers update live. A heavier shield protects you from ice but costs Δv. A thermal camera finds a clue nothing else can, but takes a sensor slot.
