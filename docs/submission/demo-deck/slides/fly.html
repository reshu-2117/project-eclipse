Brake to catch up

A lower orbit is a faster one. The game makes you feel it.

The flight runs on exact two-body orbital mechanics with the rocket equation, so a weak engine pays for long burns and a sloppy burn costs fuel later. The computer's burns come out at the Handbook's numbers, and automated tests check that every route lands on the approach point.
