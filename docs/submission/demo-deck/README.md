# Demo deck (7 slides)

The NASA Space Apps demo: a 7-slide deck (the rules allow at most 7, title included). It is published as a Slides artifact on claude.ai; this folder keeps its source so the team has it.

- `deck.json`, `deck.css`, `slides/*.html`: the deck as published. Images in the slides point at uploaded copies (`/_blob/…`); the same pictures are in `img/` (screenshots of the live game, no people, no AI imagery).
- Slides: cover (team, challenge), the mission and the hangar, flying under real gravity, the ice alert and docking, the station walk (thermal visor, the test), the NASA figures used, the debrief and links.
- For the submission the deck must be **public**: open it on claude.ai and share it from its Share menu (or export it to PDF or PowerPoint there).
